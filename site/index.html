No. 13Q & A

FAQ

Quick answers.

When does Potraces launch?
It's in beta right now. Join the waitlist — you get exactly one message when it goes live.
I earn from a few different places. Does that work?
That's the whole reason it exists. Kuih orders, gig shifts, freelance invoices — log everything and see it in one calm view.
Can I run seller mode just for Raya season?
Yes. Flip between personal and seller anytime — each space keeps its own records.
Is my data safe?
Records live on your phone by default, and only reach your own cloud if you switch backup on. We never sell your data.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Log with zero bars — it saves on your phone and syncs when the line comes back.
Is it free?
Free for the whole beta, no card. When fair pricing comes, you hear it from us first.
Is this financial advice?
No — Potraces shows your own numbers clearly so you can decide. A tracker, not an advisor.
