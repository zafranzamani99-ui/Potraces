24 KB · works on iPhone with iOS 16 or newer

Sect. 01 · Set it up

Four steps, once.

First, in the Potraces app: Settings → Money Setup → Quick Log → tap Generate my key, then Copy key. Then come back here.

1

Tap 'Add the Shortcut'

Safari downloads Potraces Quick Log to your iPhone.

2

Open the download

Tap the ↓ at the top-right of Safari, tap the file, then tap 'Add Shortcut'.

3

Run it once

Open the Shortcuts app and tap the Potraces Quick Log card. The key you copied in the app is already filled in — tap Done. The first time, iOS asks to let it connect to the internet — tap Always Allow. Then log a test expense.

4

Assign it to Back Tap

iPhone Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap → Double Tap → choose Potraces Quick Log. Now a double-tap on the back of your phone logs an expense.

Sect. 02 · If it doesn't work

Common hiccups

Q1 Nothing downloaded?

Tap 'Add the Shortcut' again — Safari sometimes needs a second tap.

Q2 'Safari cannot connect to the server'?

Check your internet connection and try the button again.

Q3 Shortcut shows an error instead of logging?

Your Quick Log key was revoked — that happens if you turned off Quick Log, turned off Cloud Backup, or signed out. In Potraces open Settings → Money Setup → Quick Log, tap Regenerate key, then Copy key. The shortcut asks for the new key on its next run — it's already pasted, just tap Done.

Q4 Haven't set up Quick Log in the app yet?

Do that first — the Potraces app is where you get the key this shortcut needs. Settings → Money Setup → Quick Log.

Set up inside the Potraces app: Settings → Money Setup → Quick Log.
